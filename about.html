About the company

Global AI banking platform for the modern world.

Scurry’s vision is to make stablecoin banking feel instant, secure, and accessible for both everyday users and global businesses.

Mission

Reimagine cross-border banking.

Scurry is focused on solving the friction of traditional cross-border banking: high fees, slow settlement, limited access, and avoidable complexity.

Lower-cost infrastructure powered by stablecoins and modern software.

Consumer and business products built for a digital-first world.

A regulatory-first approach with a long-term banking vision.

Roadmap themes

What Scurry is building toward.

Digital-only infrastructure

Mobile, web, API-friendly operations and real-time movement are core to the platform direction.

Business and consumer banking

Scurry is pursuing products for both sides of the market, from cards to treasury and transfers.

AI agent platform

Longer-term plans include AI-supported workflows across onboarding, compliance, treasury, support, and more.

Global outlook

Focused on international growth.

Scurry’s market direction emphasizes North America, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia-Pacific, with a particular focus on Mexico, LATAM, the Middle East, and Africa.
